    02 Scan your track

    Scan-start calibration (experimental)

    Only on a straight piece: measure the car's centre from the track centre, looking in its driving direction. Left is positive; right is negative. Leave blank for no calibration. Left/right hardware sign is still unverified.

    The car drives around to read the printed codes, then stops. Check that the map matches your track.

    03 Drive with your phone

    Choose Phone / QR on your car and scan its unique code. Enter your name on the phone, then choose Let in here. No setup code to type. Choose Start driving on this laptop and keep this tab open.

    Any free car: scan this and you get the next car whose driver is Phone. For a specific car, use the Code button on that car's card.
