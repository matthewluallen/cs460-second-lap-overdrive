THE INVESTIGATION OPENS THROUGH EVIDENCE
Bring an observation, its source and a question that separates plausible
explanations. The instructor reviews your evidence and may release a record
for your next interview with Rowan. Guessing passwords is not the objective.

Connection: Who can request or authorize a command, and where is that enforced?
Track: What was sensed, stored, inferred or commanded, and at what time?
Release: What exactly was reviewed, distributed and accepted?
Deal: Who controls the assets and service, and what supports their claims?

These are connected lines of inquiry, not exclusive team assignments. Your
team's assessment covers the whole proposal and explains the limits of its
tests. Every student develops substantial evidence and reasoning.
Rowan's interview is testimony. Preserve exact records and corroborate claims
with code, tests and primary sources. A fluent answer is not a new artifact.
An instructor can provide bridge evidence or an equivalent trace to maintain
progress. Keep unanswered questions visible in your final recommendation.
