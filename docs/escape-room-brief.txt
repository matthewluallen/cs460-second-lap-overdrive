Second Lap investigation rooms
Your team is trying to reach an evidence-based investment decision before the
fictional board deadline. The facilitator releases new packets as you demonstrate
concepts. Ask questions supported by evidence; there are no secret phrases.
Rooms cover protocol analysis, control logic, trust boundaries, update assurance,
adversary emulation, AI evidence handling and the final investment decision.
You can investigate repository clues in any order. Preserve full Git history.
Each room requires an artifact, an explanation and a consequence for the product.
Hints and equivalent accessible exercises are available without mastery penalty.
Only packets released to your team are in scope. Never target the course portal,
facilitator ledger or other teams to bypass an educational gate.
