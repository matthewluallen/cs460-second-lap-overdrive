Your commission from Casey and Matt

Each student receives an assigned kit, subject to the instructors' inventory and
hardware validation. Teams of three or four investigate together and present one
investment recommendation. Every student submits an individual evidence note.
The simulator provides an equivalent route when a kit or battery is unavailable.

Deliver a useful controller improvement, a reproducible security assessment,
an evidence-based rights and provenance analysis, and a recommendation to fund,
fund with conditions, pivot, or decline. You may reach different conclusions.

Maintain an evidence register: claim, source, date obtained, hash if applicable,
observation, inference, alternative explanation, confidence, next question.
Separate what you observed from what somebody told you. Preserve originals.
Do not treat a persuasive interview, an old commit date or a successful demo as
independent proof. Identify which questions require an owner, expert or counsel.

Final presentation: 8 minutes plus 5 minutes of investor questions. Show a
controlled before-and-after demonstration, identify your three material risks,
cite your strongest evidence, and state measurable funding conditions.
Each member must explain a different part of the evidence or implementation.

Authorized targets: your local simulation, your assigned course repository and
only the devices specifically assigned for supervised physical testing.
The fictional founder may disagree with you. An instructor safety instruction
always takes precedence over an in-character request. No real purchases,
contracts, public accusations, third-party account access or unsolicited contact.
